# Final Fantasy IV DS

## What does randomization do to this game?

Treasure chests, hidden items, map completion rewards, boss victories and some story events
give Archipelago items instead of their original contents.

This first version covers the beginning of the game, from Baron to Rosa being cured in Kaipo.

## What is the goal?

Cure Rosa in Kaipo with the Sand Pearl.

## Which items can be in another player's world?

Any of them, including the Sand Pearl, which is required to cure Rosa.
